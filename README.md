This project is a dedicated space for **beginners** who want to sharpen their JavaScript skills and master the essential GitHub workflow. Whether you're just starting out or looking to practice, this is for you!

> [!TIP]

## 🎮 How to Play (The Rules of 21)

Blackjack is a classic casino card game where you compete against the dealer. The goal is simple: **Get as close to 21 as possible without going over.**

| Action | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hit** | Take another card to increase your total. |
| **Stand** | Keep your current hand and end your turn. |
| **Bust** | Your total went over 21—you lose! |
| **Blackjack** | An Ace and a 10-value card (total 21)—higher payout! |

**Card Values:**
- **2 - 10**: Face value.
- **Face Cards (J, Q, K)**: Worth 10.
- **Aces**: Worth 1 or 11 (whichever helps you more).

## 🛠️ Get Started (GitHub Skills)

Ready to put your GitHub skills into practice? Follow these steps:

1.  **Fork** this repository to your own account.
2.  **Clone** your fork to your local machine.
3.  **Create** a new branch for your features.
4.  **Commit** your changes and **Push** them back to GitHub.
5.  Open a **Pull Request** to share your progress!
